# Timeline and Historical Context
![nkchronology.png|568x401](/img/user/Images/nkchronology.png)
- Dynasties are very diverse and different characteristically
- Egypt during this time is often thought of as an empire
	- Different policies in Levant and Nubia
	- Militaristic kings
- Powerful royal women
- Royal patronage of temples
- Centralized kingship
	- Super strong in Dynasty 18-19
	- Weakens in Dynasty 20, leading to Third Intermediate Period
