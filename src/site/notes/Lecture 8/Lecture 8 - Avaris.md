# Overview of Avaris
- Founded in Dynasty 12
- Same city style as [[Lecture 7/Lecture 7 - Abydos in the Middle Kingdom#^1def76\|Lahun]]
	- Over 300 houses
- Melting pot of culture
- No break in material culture in the transition from MK to SIP

	- Shows relationships with other peoples

### Architectural Evidence
- Construction style
	- Similar to Lahun
	- Means that this was constructed by native Egyptians
	- Probably more peaceful/trade-centered relationships
![palaceconstructavaris.png|572x399](/img/user/Images/palaceconstructavaris.png)
- Palace-style construction
	- Blend of Egyptian and Levantine construction
		- Uses the Hekau-khasut to tell that he is a ruler of the Hyksos
		- Also uses traditional Egyptian symbols of power/kingship

## Burial of 'Amu
![amuburial.png|546x357](/img/user/Images/amuburial.png)
- Name \'Amu literally means "The Asiatic"
- Deputy treasurer at Avaris
- Mixed burial styles, with both Levantine and Egyptian practices
	- Buried with donkeys (Levantine)
	- Mudbrick subterranean chamber (Levantine)
	- Egyptian pots (Egyptian)
	- Foreign name (Levantine)
	- Egyptian burial (Egyptian)
	- Egyptian titles (Egyptian)
