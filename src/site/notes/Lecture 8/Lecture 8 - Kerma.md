## Royal Burials
![kermaburials.png|593x356](/img/user/Images/kermaburials.png)
- Royal tumulus
	- Basically just a large mound
	- Burial is inside of the mound
	- Characteristic of kingship or power
- Bucrania
	- Using cow skulls for burial
	- Ritual in nature
	- Respect, importance of the person being buried
