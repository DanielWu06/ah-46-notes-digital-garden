# Erasure by Thutmose III
{ #345dc1}


![erasureofhatshepsut.png|547x291](/img/user/erasureofhatshepsut.png)
- Thutmose III and Hatshepsut are co-regents for 20 years
	- While alive, Hatshepsut was significantly more important or powerful
- Doesn't start immediately
	- Only begins in year 42 of his reign (~22 years after Hatshepsut's death)
	- Probably realized that his legacy wouldn't look as impressive as Hatshepsut
- Doesn't erase everything
	- Only images of her as king
	- Not any images of her alone
- Removal in writing
	- Replaces her name
		- Not with his, but Tuthmosis I or Tuthmosis II
- Purpose?
	- Legitimize himself and legacy
	- Legitimize his son
		- Since they weren't married, he wanted to replace her with his own lineage
		- Worried her daughter would be seen as the legitimate ruler
