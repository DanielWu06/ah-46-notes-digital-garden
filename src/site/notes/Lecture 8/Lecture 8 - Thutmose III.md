# Tomb in the Valley of the Kings
![thutmoseIIItomb.png|438x252](/img/user/thutmoseIIItomb.png)
- Depictions on wall
	- 12 hours of the night
	- Sun god's trials and tribulations in nighttime
- Purpose of depictions
	- Sun rising and setting symbolized rebirth
# The Akh-Menu at Karnak
![karnakthutmoseIIIakhmenu.png|280x268](/img/user/karnakthutmoseIIIakhmenu.png)
- Although Karnak was for divine temples, this was NOT for gods
	- Instead, it was a celebration of kingship
- King list
	- Included kings from FIP and SIP
		- Very unsuualy
	- Did not include Hatshepsut
		- Probably related to [[Lecture 8/Lecture 8 - Hatshepsut#^345dc1\|his erasure of her legacy as king]]

# Contra-temple at Karnak
![karnakcontratemple.png|434x320](/img/user/karnakcontratemple.png)
- Space reserved for the common people
- Unlike the restrictive divine temples, people came and went as they pleased

# Thutmose III as the Warrior-King
![thutmoseIIIwarrior.png|183x217](/img/user/thutmoseIIIwarrior.png)
- Known as a warrior king
- At least 17 pushes into the Levant
- Personally led Egyptian army
- Known for conquest of the Levant
	- Battle of Megiddo
