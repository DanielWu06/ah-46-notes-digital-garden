# Historical Context
## Middle Kingdom Decline
![mkdecline.png|275x417](/img/user/Images/mkdecline.png)
- Dynasty 13 moves power center from Itjtawy to Thebes
- Egypt loses control over Memphite area
- Tell el-Dab'a/Avaris (a middle kingdom site in the delta) becomes inhabited by Asiatics who form Dynasty 14 and 15
	- Hyksos gain power

## Second Intermediate Period
![SIPmappower.png|224x460](/img/user/Images/SIPmappower.png)
- After MK, north fell to Hyksos control
	- Dynasties 14 and 15
- Native Egyptian rulers congregated around Thebes
	- Dynasties 16 and 17
- No clear sequence of kings
	- Ruled together
	- Similar to confusions of FIP
- Kingdom of Kush rising in the south
	- Actual evidence of conflict now
	- Different from lack of evidence of violence in MK
- Egypt reunified by Theban Dynasty 17, war against Hyksos
	- Dynasty 18 begins new kingdom
		- Also fighting against Kush
