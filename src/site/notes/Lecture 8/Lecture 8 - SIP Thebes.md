- We know a few of the kings
- Less impressive than Avaris and Kerma
	- Likely focused on reuinification

# SIP Thebes Burial Practices

- Inclusion of pyramids on tombs

## Religious Practices
- Building of temples and divine monuments continued
- Relationship between kingship and gods remained relatively strong
