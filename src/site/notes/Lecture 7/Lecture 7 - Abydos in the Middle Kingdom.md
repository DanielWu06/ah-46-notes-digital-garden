# Changes in Abydos
- In the Middle Kingdom, Abydos once again becomes an important site
- Khentiamentiu and Osiris
	- Local god named Khentiamentiu
	- Eventually, the two are syncretized (merged)
		- Osiris effectively becomes the local god
- Divine processions of Osiris
	- States taken out of the shrines and brought around the cliffs/area
	- Began in Umm el-Qaab, went to tomb of king/Osiris
	- Believed to be almost performative, or in some sense a performance/festival

## Osiris's Death and Tomb
- Osiris was thought to have died
	- Osiris myth
	- Now serves as ruler of the Duat, land of the dead
- Thought to be the his burial place
	- Brought legitimacy and a connection to the divine
	- Tomb of Osiris => Tomb of first dynasty king
- Important for the procession

## Middle Kingdom Coffins
![mkeyes.png|627x371](/img/user/Images/mkeyes.png)
- Eyes become a big motif in middle kingdom funerary goods
	- Kind of human, but not really
	- Called Eyes of Horus (sometimes Ra)
		- Composite of human and falcon eyes
	- Served as protection for the body inside
- Microcosms of the tomb as a whole
	- Often had pictures of items
		- Weapons
		- Goods
		- Etc
	- Often, depicted items or things had physical versions were used in ritual
- Also had coffin texts
	- Evolved from [[Lecture 4/Lecture 4 - Pyramid of Unas#^80dac4\|pyramid texts]]
	- Book of Two Ways
		- Rivers, topographical features, etc
		- Supposed to be a mapping of the afterlife

## Middle Kingdom Funerary Masks
![funerarymasks.png|573x417](/img/user/Images/funerarymasks.png)
- Not very realistic
	- Weird proportions
- Gold/Green
	- Typically, men are red and women yellow
		- So, this does not follow typical conventions
	- Gold was associated gods/deification
	- Green was associated with Osiris
	- Therefore, these people were deified in death
- Deification
	- In the Middle Kingdom, it was common belief for the dead to be deified
	- "Becoming AN Osiris"
		- Not THE Osiris, more just a sign of divinity
	- One way to survive the afterlife
